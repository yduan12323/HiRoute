Conclusion to test: quantify candidate reduction and the distribution of decision
loss, without selecting an epsilon configuration to force Go-1 success.

Evidence: primary all-attached, no-dwell scenarios; all 30 ODs, four practical
budgets, seven primary tasks, sixteen fixed evaluation vectors. Aggregate only
with explicit median/p95 definitions, and accompany averages with task tables,
missing-oracle counts and infinite-loss counts. Parking-only is secondary.

Archetype: quantitative grid for compression/regret and recommendation coverage;
asymmetric mixed-modality diagnostics for successful, worst-loss and early
unstable cases. Maps illustrate recorded candidate selections, not correctness.

Backend: Python/matplotlib only, matching the repository workflow. Editable
SVG/PDF and 300 dpi PNG diagnostics, approximately 183 mm width. TIFF and
submission-specific author-guide conformity are outside this diagnostic export.
No statistical significance or population inference is claimed.

Data integrity: every primary evaluation enters table summaries. No point
sampling. Finite-loss distributions explicitly identify infinite losses and
absent flat oracles. Maps may zoom locally and select task-relevant capabilities;
these selections and case identities are saved. Road-node polylines are
structural diagnostic geometry, not certified entrance routes.
